Methodology

Era-aware z-normalised scoring

BRR and BRSR have structurally different indicator sets. Mixing eras would confound the index. EDQI scores each era independently, then merges the panel.

01
Era detection
Fiscal year ≥ 2022 → ERA 2 (BRSR, 32 indicators). Fiscal year < 2022 → ERA 1 (BRR, 25 indicators). Each era's years are scored only against era peers.
02
Z-normalisation within era
Each indicator is z-scored across all years within the same era (μ and σ computed from that era's data), then min-max scaled to [0, 100].
03
Inversion for lower-is-better indicators
GHG emissions, incidents, fines, and similar indicators are inverted after scaling so that 100 always represents best practice, not highest value.
04
Pillar aggregation and composite
E, S, and G scores are the unweighted mean of their member indicators. EDQI composite = (E + S + G) / 3. Coverage % tracks successfully extracted indicators.
EDQI Formula
z_i= (raw_i − μ_era) / σ_era
s_i= minmax(z_i) × 100
if inv: s_i = 100 − s_i
E_score= mean(s_i) for E
S_score= mean(s_i) for S
G_score= mean(s_i) for G
EDQI= (E + S + G) / 3

Indicator System

57 KPIs across two regulatory eras

ERA 1 covers the Business Responsibility Report framework (FY 2012–2021). ERA 2 covers the expanded Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (FY 2022 onwards).
