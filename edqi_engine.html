Scoring Methodology

Era-aware z-normalised scoring

BRR and BRSR are structurally different regulatory frameworks. Scoring ERA 1 years against ERA 2 peers would confound the index. EDQI scores each era independently, then merges the result into a unified panel.

01
PDF text extraction
Each annual report PDF is parsed with pdfplumber. A keyword-density sliding window (9,000 chars) identifies the ESG-dense section — the segment most likely to contain BRR or BRSR disclosures — and passes it to the LLM.
02
LLM extraction via structured prompt
A structured prompt tuned to the fiscal year's regulatory era asks the model for exactly 25 (ERA 1) or 32 (ERA 2) numeric KPIs in JSON. Non-disclosed indicators return null.
03
Within-era z-normalisation
Each indicator is z-normalised across all years within the same era, then min-max scaled to [0, 100]. Lower-is-better indicators (emissions, incidents, fines) are inverted so 100 always means best practice.
04
Pillar aggregation and composite
E, S, and G pillar scores are the unweighted mean of their member indicator scores. The EDQI composite is (E + S + G) / 3. Coverage % tracks how many indicators were successfully extracted.
EDQI Formula
z_i = (raw_i − μ_era) / σ_era
s_i = minmax(z_i) × 100
if inv: s_i = 100 − s_i
E_score = mean(s_i) for E indicators
S_score = mean(s_i) for S indicators
G_score = mean(s_i) for G indicators
EDQI = (E + S + G) / 3
Scope ERA 1 ↔ ERA 2 scored
independently
